There once was a fellow named Clyde,
Who found a fast horse he could ride.
But when it took flight,
He held on so tight,
That now he just glides far and wide.

A young girl who lived near the bay,
Would sing to the fish every day.
But one stormy night,
She gave them a fright,
And they all swam in terror away.

There once was a cat from Peru,
Who danced in a pink tutu.
It pranced and it twirled,
With ribbons unfurled,
And meowed, “I belong in a zoo!”

A baker from London named Lou,
Once slipped and fell into his stew.
He climbed from the pot,
Exclaiming, "It's hot!"
And now he just sticks to fondue.

A scientist working in space,
Dropped his lunch—it just floated in place!
He tried to retrieve,
But had to believe,
That tacos in zero-G chase.

A pirate who lived on the sea,
Had a parrot who mimicked with glee.
One day it did swear,
Gave the captain a scare,
Now it lives in a library, free.

A penguin who loved to play ball,
Was known as the best of them all.
It kicked and it slid,
Like no other did,
And danced when it made the crowd call. :)
